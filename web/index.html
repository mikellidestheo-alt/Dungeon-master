How to play: pick Dig, drag over earth/gold to tag it (right-drag untags). Imps dig, mine, claim floor and carry gold home. Build a Treasury to bank gold and a Lair so creatures from the portal (far right, claim it!) stop getting angry. Heroes: dig a tunnel from your dungeon to the red-tinted Hero Gate (south) and invasion waves start — Knights, Archers and Mages hunt your Heart. Use ✋ Hand to pick up an imp or creature and drop it into the fight. Creatures heal and level up from kills; imps flee. Sound: everything is synthesised in your browser (no audio files). It starts at your first click; use the speaker button or M to mute, the sliders for master, music and effects volume. Constants come from OpenKeeper's level variables; see docs/.
