        Your dungeon: the Dig / grab tool tags earth and gold as you drag (right-drag untags) and picks up a creature you click; right-click cancels any other tool. Build a Treasury to bank it and a Lair for beds, then claim the purple Portal: creatures come (Monsters > Who will come), as many as your Lair and Hatchery can keep.
        The invasion starts when a tunnel links a Hero Gate to your Heart, so you choose when: dig all but the last tile, set doors and traps, then open it. Commands are in the tabs: Buildings (B), Spells (C, or 1-5) and Monsters (H, with the ✋ Hand).
        Winning: finish the objectives (top left) and keep your Heart alive. ☰ Chapters is the menu; locked items are researched in a Library. Pan: W A S D or the edge; F is full screen.
