How the sandbox works

  • The map. A large dungeon (88 × 60 tiles): your Dungeon Heart in the west, the Portal in the east and up to six Hero Gates round the edge (south, north, south-east, north-east, south-west, north-west), so heroes can come from different directions. The seed decides the rock and the gold, so the same seed is the same dungeon every time.
  • You start the invasion. Nothing happens until a tunnel links a Gate to your Heart; then the countdown to the first wave begins. Dig most of the tunnel, set doors and traps, and open the last tile when you are ready.
  • A fixed siege is won by beating every wave. It gives no stars and does not unlock or change any chapter.
  • Endless has no victory. Waves grow stronger and come faster, and a boss leads every fifth one (the Prince, then the King). Press Retire in the top bar to bank your score, or hold on until the Heart falls. The score is the waves you survived.
  • Difficulty sets hero strength, the countdown before the first wave, the gap between waves, the Heart's health and how many heroes dig their own tunnels to reach your creatures (none for Apprentice, every hero for Dungeon Master). Without Everything researched you must study Prison, Temple, traps and spells in a Library.
  • Playing works as in the chapters: tag earth with Dig, build on claimed floor, keep your creatures housed, fed and paid, and keep imps alive to re-arm traps. The README has the full guide.
